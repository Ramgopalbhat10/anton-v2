# Web UI and notifications

Linked from [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `src/web/` — TanStack Router UI; shadcn components in `components/ui/`, motion tokens in `instrument.css`.
- Look: charcoal greys (canvas `#0e0e0e`, surfaces stepping up by fill) and frosted glass over a sky, one cyan accent (`--cyan-*` in `styles/signal-tokens.css`). The sky is an hourly NASA picture (`lib/sky.ts`, crops in `public/sky/`) drawn as a dithered pixel mesh (`components/sky.tsx`); `<main data-sky>` veils it per page (Home most, the artifact viewer least, every other page alike), its menu on Home swaps in a random one of the 24 until the top of the hour (`shuffleSky`, kept in this browser) or switches to NASA's picture of the day, Settings › General turns it off or picks the mode, a Brightness slider in both places (`SkyBrightnessSlider`, `anton.sky.brightness`, 0–100 with 60 as drawn) fades the picture below the default and brightens and saturates it above (`skyLook`), and `?sky=<slug>` pins one.
- The picture of the day comes through the `DailyPicture` port (`src/providers/nasa/apod.ts`: the APOD feed on science.nasa.gov, api.nasa.gov with `NASA_API_KEY` as the fallback). `src/services/sky.ts` asks once a day and keeps the entry and a web-sized copy in memory and under `data/sky/`; `GET /api/sky/today` names it and `GET /api/sky/today/image` serves it, so the browser never calls NASA.
- Scales: a size, a corner or a fill comes from a step in `styles/signal-tokens.css`, never from a value typed by hand (`tests/ui/design-tokens.test.tsx` fails on `text-[13px]`, `rounded-[6px]`, Tailwind's own `text-sm` / `rounded-md`, and a hex colour outside a drawing). Text has nine steps (`text-nano` 10, `text-micro` 11, `text-small` 12, `text-body` 13, `text-body-lg` 14, `text-h3` 16, `text-h2` 20, `text-h1` 24, `text-display` 32); a size sets no line height, so `leading-*` goes beside it. Corners have six (`rounded-mark` 2, `rounded-inner` 4, `rounded-control` 6, `rounded-tile` 8, `rounded-card` 12, `rounded-dialog` 16) and `rounded-full`. Fills have five (`--bg-inset`, `--bg-canvas`, `--bg-surface`, `--bg-raised`, `--bg-overlay`); `--card-bg`, `--well-bg` and `--pop-bg` are names for steps on that ramp, not colours of their own. `cn` (`lib/utils.ts`) knows the step names, so a size merges with a colour and one radius replaces another; import it from there. Keyboard focus is one ring everywhere: `--focus-ring`, an accent edge with a soft wash.
- Controls: one of each, so a pick looks the same on every screen. `Tabs`/`Tab` (`components/instrument.tsx`) for the sections of a page, on a rule; `PanelTabs`/`PanelTab` for open panels and open files at the top of a pane; `Segmented`/`SegmentedItem` for two to four ways to show the same thing; `FilterChip` (`components/signal.tsx`) for filters that toggle; `SearchField` for every search box. Whatever is chosen (a tab, a segment, a chip, the open file or task) takes `.in-chosen`: the `--bg-active` fill and primary text, a clear step above a hover's `--bg-hover`; the accent is kept for focus and for what wants you. A state is `StatusDot` (grey off, accent live, attention waiting, amber caution, red failed), or `Status` for a dot and a word. Group labels are sentence case (`SectionLabel`, the sidebar's `GroupHeading`); uppercase is only for keys. An empty state is sized by what holds it: `EmptyState` with `art` for a panel or page, with an `icon` or words alone in a card or tab, `EmptyLine` in a list. `components/ui/` keeps only the shadcn pieces in use (input, input-group, button under it, popover, select, textarea, toggle-group, toggle under it, tooltip); build with `signal.tsx` and `instrument.tsx` first.
- Type: Geist everywhere; Geist Mono only for code (code blocks, diffs, file views, the terminal, code-like inputs). Numbers that line up use `.in-num` (tabular figures), not the mono face.
- Every clickable thing shows the pointer cursor (one rule in `styles.css`). Tooltips are the shared dark tip with no arrow: `ui/tooltip.tsx` for radix ones, and `components/title-tips.tsx` draws the same tip for any plain `title` attribute. Sticky headers inside popovers and panels use `.sticky-veil`, which fills with the surface's own `--surface-fill` (set by `.in-pop` and `.settings-card`), so they are the same colour as the surface and hide what scrolls under them. Popovers and menus are solid (`.in-pop`, also on the `ui/` popover and select).
- Cards have no edge: they read by fill, one step above what they sit on (`--card-bg` over `--bg-canvas`), with a slight lift (`--card-shadow` = `--elev-1`). Shadows come only from the four steps in `styles/signal-tokens.css`: `--elev-1` cards and tiles, `--elev-2` the Home composer and panels and glass, `--elev-3` popovers, menus, hover cards and tips, `--elev-4` modals and the settings card. Each is a faint light line along the top inside (light from above), a tight contact shadow, and from step 2 a short soft spread; never a long drop, and no new raw `rgba` shadows in components. The border stays transparent so sizes never shift; never an outer ring (a scroll container clips it); the only inset line is the elevation's faint top light. `.in-card` (`--radius-card`) for titled cards, `.in-tile` (`--radius-tile`) for small ones (a reply's files, steps, thread cards); both turn to frosted glass over the sky (`--glass-bg`, `--glass-blur`) with the same lift. A card whose rows or link take a hover fill clips them (`overflow-hidden`) so the fill keeps the card's corners. Tips (`.tip`) size to their text (`width: max-content`), so one near the window's edge never shrinks to a column. Illustration wells (`.in-grid`) have no flat fill, only washes that fade before the edge, so they never show as a box.
- Pull request colours are their own: `--pr-*` tokens (open green, draft grey, merged violet, closed red, conflicts orange; checks passed green, running amber, failing red), read through `prTone()` / `prColor()` in `components/pr-status.tsx` wherever a PR's state shows. Icons sit bare, without a tile behind them; only a subagent's role glyph is coloured.
- Illustrations are drawn the way the rack is (`illustrations.tsx`): flat trays and slim tiles, short rules, one light, greys plus the one accent. The mark (`Logo`), the mascot and the loader are Anton's cyan cube; run `node scripts/make-icons.ts` after changing the mark.
- Settings is a card over the page you are on (`components/settings/modal.tsx`, state in `lib/settings-modal.ts`). It runs the settings pages on its own in-memory router; the `/settings` route only opens the card and goes back, and a link inside the card to anywhere else closes it and goes there in the app. A project's settings open the same way (`ProjectSettingsModal` in `components/project-settings.tsx`, state in `lib/project-settings-modal.ts`); `/projects/<id>/settings` opens the card over that project.
- States stay calm: done is quiet white, anything that wants you takes the accent (`--attention-*`, the `attention` tone), a caution is amber (`--warning-*`: a cap, something unavailable, unpushed work), only a failure is red (`--danger-*`). Diffs keep green and red (`--diff-add-*`, `--diff-remove-*`).
- New tasks and threads open the moment you send, by an id the page chose (`src/web/lib/starting.ts`, `starting-task.tsx` draws the skeleton).
- Pages refetch on server events mapped in `src/web/lib/live-updates.ts`; keep sidebar rows cheap (`sameRow`).
- On a phone-width screen (`useOnePane`) a task opens on its conversation; side panels open on demand.
- `src/services/push.ts` — Web Push notifications through the `PushSender` port (`src/providers/webpush/sender.ts`), never while a page says it is in front of you. Service worker: `src/web/public/sw.js`.
- `src/services/notifications.ts` — the Notifications dialog (bell left of the sidebar toggle, `components/notifications.tsx`): only moments worth a look, each a row keyed by what it stands for in a task, so a newer one replaces it. A reply ends in one row (asked you, plan or call to approve, replied, done, failed), its sandbox in another (created, stopped once idle, failed to start) and its pull request in a third (checks failed, merged). Sandbox rows stay out of the bell's count; opening a task reads its rows.
