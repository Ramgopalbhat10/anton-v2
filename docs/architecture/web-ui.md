# Web UI and notifications

Linked from [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `src/web/` — TanStack Router UI; shadcn components in `components/ui/`. The design system is Aura: tokens in `styles/aura-tokens.css`, component classes in `styles/aura-components.css` and `styles/instrument.css`.
- Look: cool zinc neutrals and one indigo accent (`--accent`, `#6e72f7`), kept for an action or live agent work. Tokens come in two tiers: primitives (`--n-*`, `--a-*`, the state hues) and the semantic names components read (`--bg-*`, `--line*`, `--text*`, `--accent*`, `--ok`/`--warn`/`--bad`/`--info`, `--merged`, `--conflict`); a component never reads a primitive. Dark is `:root`; `[data-theme='light']` gives the same names paper values. There is no picture behind the app: structure is hairlines, and every surface is a plain fill.
- Scales: a size, a corner or a fill comes from a step in `styles/aura-tokens.css`, never from a value typed by hand (`tests/ui/design-tokens.test.tsx` fails on `text-[13px]`, `rounded-[6px]`, Tailwind's own `text-sm` / `rounded-md`, and a hex colour outside a drawing). Text has nine steps (`text-nano` 10, `text-micro` 11, `text-small` 12, `text-body` 13, `text-body-lg` 14, `text-h3` 16, `text-h2` 20, `text-h1` 24, `text-display` 32); a size sets no line height, so `leading-*` goes beside it. Corners have five (`rounded-mark` 3, `rounded-inner` 4 for badges, keys and menu items, `rounded-control` 6 for controls and inputs, `rounded-card` 8 for cards, panels and popovers, `rounded-dialog` 12 for dialogs and the composer) and `rounded-full`. Fills have five, each a step forward (`--bg-app` the window and sidebar, `--bg-panel` the content panel, `--bg-surface` cards and wells, `--bg-raised` inputs and cards on cards, `--bg-overlay` menus, popovers and dialogs); `--bg-hover` and `--bg-active` are translucent, so they hold on any of them. Controls are 28px high, 24 in dense rows and toolbars, 32 for a page's main action (`--h-md`, `--h-sm`, `--h-lg`). `cn` (`lib/utils.ts`) knows the step names, so a size merges with a colour and one radius replaces another; import it from there. Keyboard focus is one ring everywhere: `--focus`, or `--focus-inset` where a ring would be clipped.
- Controls: one of each, so a pick looks the same on every screen. `Tabs`/`Tab` (`components/instrument.tsx`) for the sections of a page, on a rule; `PanelTabs`/`PanelTab` for open panels and open files at the top of a pane; `Segmented`/`SegmentedItem` for two to four ways to show the same thing (the chosen one is a raised key, `.in-segment-on`); `FilterChip` (`components/signal.tsx`) for filters that toggle (a hairline chip; the accent's soft fill and line when on); `SearchField` for every search box. Whatever is chosen (a tab, a segment, a chip, the open file or task) takes `.in-chosen`: the `--bg-active` fill and primary text, a clear step above a hover's `--bg-hover`; the accent is kept for focus, an action and live work. A state is `StatusDot` (grey off, accent live, amber waiting or a caution, red failed), or `Status` for a dot and a word. Group labels are sentence case (`SectionLabel`, the sidebar's `GroupHeading`); uppercase is only for keys. An empty state is sized by what holds it: `EmptyState` with `art` for a panel or page, with an `icon` or words alone in a card or tab, `EmptyLine` in a list. `components/ui/` keeps only the shadcn pieces in use (input, input-group, button under it, popover, select, textarea, toggle-group, toggle under it, tooltip); build with `signal.tsx` and `instrument.tsx` first.
- Type: Inter everywhere, in three weights (400 reading, 500 actions and labels, 600 hierarchy); JetBrains Mono only for what the agent reads and writes (code blocks, diffs, file views, the terminal, paths, branch names, code-like inputs). Numbers that line up use `.in-num` (tabular figures), not the mono face. Buttons are `Btn` (`signal.tsx`, `.sg-btn`): `primary` in the accent for the one main action, `secondary` raised with a line, `ghost`, `inverse`, `danger`. Text fields share `.sg-field` (the shadcn input, textarea, select and `SearchField`). Badges (`Badge`, `Chip`, a PR's flag) are 18px high with a soft fill of their tone and no edge.
- Shell (`shell.tsx`, `chat-sidebar.tsx`): the window is the canvas (`--bg-app`). The sidebar sits on it, 236px wide with 8px of padding and no edge of its own; every page is drawn in one work panel beside it (`.app-main`: `--bg-panel`, 8px from the window's edge, an 8px corner and a hairline). Collapsed, the sidebar leaves a 48px rail; on a phone it is a sheet over the page and the panel is the whole window. Sidebar rows are 28px (`NAV_ROW`; a task row with its second line is 40px), the chosen one on `--bg-active`; a group's actions (`GroupHeading`) show on hover or focus.
- Pages: every page opens under a 44px top bar (`TopBar` in `page-frame.tsx`): where you are as crumbs, the last one bright, and the page's actions on the right. Home (`launcher.tsx`, `home-dashboard.tsx`) is a launcher, not a dashboard: a greeting with how many tasks wait on you and how many run, the composer, then only what wants you (Needs you, each row with the step that moves it on; Running; the latest few) and one quiet strip of figures (this week's tasks and merges, the month's spend, a bar for each of the last 14 days), all in a 760px column. A composer (`.composer`, `.home-composer`) is the one surface with a 12px corner; its pickers are quiet pills (`PickerChip`, the model picker's trigger), plan mode is a pill that takes the accent's soft fill when on, and send is the inverse button. A list page (`PageFrame`: Tasks, Projects, Reviews, Artifacts) opens on its name as an `h1` with a line on what it is for, as a settings page does; the bar above it is only for a phone. A task opens with its pinned panels; with none, the workspace is a slim list of panels to open (`WorkspacePane narrow`) and the conversation keeps the room. In a reply, a table keeps its columns (cells wrap between words, code in a cell never breaks) and scrolls sideways. A notification shows its body as plain words (`plainPreview` in `lib/format.ts`).
- Every clickable thing shows the pointer cursor (one rule in `styles.css`). Tooltips are the shared dark tip with no arrow: `ui/tooltip.tsx` for radix ones, and `components/title-tips.tsx` draws the same tip for any plain `title` attribute. Sticky headers inside popovers and panels use `.sticky-veil`, which fills with the surface's own `--surface-fill` (set by `.in-pop` and `.settings-card`), so they are the same colour as the surface and hide what scrolls under them. Popovers and menus are solid (`.in-pop`, also on the `ui/` popover and select).
- Hairlines make structure; shadows only say which layers float. A card is a fill one step above what it sits on with a 1px line drawn inside it (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line)`), so the line never changes a size and a scroll container never clips it: `.in-card` for titled cards and `.in-tile` for small ones (a reply's files, steps, thread cards), both `--r-8`. A rule between rows or sections is a `border-(--line)`. Shadows come only from the steps in `styles/aura-tokens.css`: `--sh-1` a control's or the panel's finish, `--sh-2` popovers, menus, hover cards and tips, `--sh-3` dialogs and the settings card, `--sh-composer` under the composer; `--sh-2` and `--sh-3` carry their own edge, so a floating layer has no border. No new raw `rgba` shadows in components. A card whose rows or link take a hover fill clips them (`overflow-hidden`) so the fill keeps the card's corners. Tips (`.tip`) size to their text (`width: max-content`), so one near the window's edge never shrinks to a column. Illustration wells (`.in-grid`) have no flat fill, only washes that fade before the edge, so they never show as a box.
- Pull request colours are their own: open green (`--ok`), draft grey, merged violet (`--merged`), closed red (`--bad`), conflicts orange (`--conflict`); checks passed green, running amber, failing red. They are read through `prTone()`, `prColor()` and `prChipStyle()` in `components/pr-status.tsx` wherever a PR's state shows. Icons are Lucide at a 1.75 stroke and sit bare, without a tile behind them; only a subagent's role glyph is coloured.
- Illustrations are drawn the way the rack is (`illustrations.tsx`): flat trays and slim tiles, short rules, one light, greys plus the one accent. The mark (`Logo`) is a ring and its centre on a tile of the accent; run `node scripts/make-icons.ts` after changing it, which redraws the favicon and app icons and restamps their links. A state is one drawn mark everywhere (`Glyph` in `thread-glyph.tsx`), told apart by shape as well as colour: a dashed ring queued, an accent ring with a turning wedge working, an amber ring and dot waiting on you, a violet ring three quarters full in review, a green ring with a check approved, a solid green check done, solid red with a bang failed, a grey ring idle.
- Settings is a card over the page you are on (`components/settings/modal.tsx`, state in `lib/settings-modal.ts`). It runs the settings pages on its own in-memory router; the `/settings` route only opens the card and goes back, and a link inside the card to anywhere else closes it and goes there in the app. A project's settings open the same way (`ProjectSettingsModal` in `components/project-settings.tsx`, state in `lib/project-settings-modal.ts`); `/projects/<id>/settings` opens the card over that project.
- States stay calm: done is quiet, live agent work takes the accent, what waits on you or is a caution is amber (`--warn`, the `attention` and `warning` tones: a question, a cap, something unavailable, unpushed work), only a failure is red (`--bad`). Diffs keep green and red (`--diff-add`, `--diff-del`).
- New tasks and threads open the moment you send, by an id the page chose (`src/web/lib/starting.ts`, `starting-task.tsx` draws the skeleton).
- Pages refetch on server events mapped in `src/web/lib/live-updates.ts`; keep sidebar rows cheap (`sameRow`), and the sidebar's Recent draws 50 rows at a time (Show more; the Tasks page lists all). A model turn sends `usage` (context and spend only), each tool call `files`, a reply starting or ending `state`. Only an open query refetches, so a query a page reads all the time must not be one every tool call marks stale: the thread's file links read the cached file list and fetch it when the page opens and when a reply starts or ends.
- A task's queries come from `taskQueries` in `src/web/lib/task-queries.ts`, never an inline `queryFn`: the query cache keeps the last reader's options for `gcTime`, and an arrow written in a component would keep that component's whole scope, and the page's DOM, alive after you leave. Hooks that hold a DOM node in a ref (`useFollowLatest`) clear it on cleanup.
- A long conversation stays cheap: finished rows carry `.thread-row-settled` (`content-visibility: auto`), so off-screen ones skip layout and paint, and "Go to input" lands on the message once the rows it scrolled past are drawn.
- Code loads as it is needed: Home and a task are in the first download; other pages (`lazyRouteComponent` in `main.tsx`), most workspace panels (`lazy` in `vm-panel.tsx`), and the Settings and project settings cards (`settings/card.tsx`, `project-settings-modal.tsx`) load the first time they open. Keep what the first page needs out of those modules: the command palette reads `settings/section-list.ts`, the launcher `settings/general-settings.ts`.
- A conversation redraws on every streamed chunk; what sits beside it is memoized so it does not (`ChatNavigation` compares your messages, `JumpToLatest`, `Waiting`, `ReadNotificationsOf`, the mascot's figure, the sidebar opener in `NavContext`). A disclosure that follows a step's running state sets it while drawing, not from an effect.
- Long lists draw what changes: the model picker's rows are memoized with handlers that never change (a pointer move redraws two rows) and open on their first 40, the rest following in a transition; an Agents lane is plain bars under one hover card that follows the pointer.
- A part that throws while drawing shows a notice in its place (`ErrorBoundary`, `LazyPart` in `components/error-boundary.tsx`) instead of taking the app down: the sidebar, each workspace panel, the message box, each reply, and the dialogs. A part whose code is gone after a release offers Reload.
- On a phone-width screen (`useOnePane`) a task opens on its conversation; side panels open on demand.
- `src/services/push.ts` — Web Push notifications through the `PushSender` port (`src/providers/webpush/sender.ts`), never while a page says it is in front of you. Service worker: `src/web/public/sw.js`.
- `src/services/notifications.ts` — the Notifications dialog (bell left of the sidebar toggle, `components/notifications.tsx`): only moments worth a look, each a row keyed by what it stands for in a task, so a newer one replaces it. A reply ends in one row (asked you, plan or call to approve, replied, done, failed), its sandbox in another (created, stopped once idle, failed to start) and its pull request in a third (checks failed, merged). Sandbox rows stay out of the bell's count; opening a task reads its rows.
