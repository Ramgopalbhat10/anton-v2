# Web UI and notifications

The TanStack Router UI, its panels, and push notifications. Linked from [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- `src/web/` — TanStack Router UI. A task or thread opens its workspace on the panels pinned in the panel menu (`pinned-panels.ts`, kept in the browser), or on the list of panels when none is pinned. New task, the command palette and New thread open the task or thread the moment you send, by an id the page chose (`id` on `POST /api/sessions` and `POST /api/spaces/:id/threads`, never one a task has or had): `src/web/lib/starting.ts` lists it everywhere tasks are listed (a frame after its page draws, so the send paints first) and `starting-task.tsx` draws its page as a skeleton until the server has made it, or says why it could not with Try again. What you asked stays on screen from the skeleton until the conversation lists it (`openingPrompt`), and a new task's first message goes as its history loads rather than after. A message sent with files shows at once while they upload (`useSends` in `sent-text.tsx`), and your own sends rise in (`in-sent`, with `in-enter` and `in-row-enter` in `instrument.css`: opacity and transforms on `--duration-sheet` and `--ease-out`, on mount only). The sidebar's rows draw again only when their task's fields change (`sameRow`; a task added above gives every row below a new object) and make their actions and menu when the pointer or focus first reaches them. `startThread` answers once the thread has its slot (`wait: false` from the page and the coordinator, shown working from then on) and hands the brief over behind the answer, since that waits on the agent's setup.
- `src/services/push.ts` — notifications with no tab open: each device that turns them on in Settings › Notifications (`settings/notifications.tsx`, `src/web/lib/push.ts`) is kept with its push subscription, and at the end of a Coder reply (`replyNotice`) or a failed one (`recordFailedReply`) every device hears that the task needs you (a question or a plan to approve), failed, or (when asked) finished. Sent through the `PushSender` port (`src/providers/webpush/sender.ts`: Web Push encryption and VAPID with no library, keys made once and kept in settings), never while a page says it is in front of you (`/api/push/presence`). The service worker (`src/web/public/sw.js`) shows them and opens the task; the manifest and icons there (`scripts/make-icons.ts`) make Anton installable. On a phone-width screen (`useOnePane`) a task, thread or project opens on its conversation and a side panel shows only once you open it.
