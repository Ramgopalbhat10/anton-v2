NASA Image of the Day

The app checks NASA’s official Image of the Day daily. The footer identifies the displayed image with its actual publication date, source link, full credit and usage terms. NASA may not publish a new image every calendar day; this is not an hourly feed or APOD.

NASA hosting alone does not establish reuse rights. Images with missing or unrecognized credits, restrictive copyright notices or captions indicating identifiable people are not displayed. When an item is unsuitable, or NASA is unavailable, Anton keeps the previous usable image or uses the bundled fallback below.

Relevant policies: NASA media guidelines, NASA/STScI/ESA/CSA content-use policy, and ESA/Hubble usage policy (CC BY 4.0). NASA material is generally not subject to copyright in the United States, but third-party restrictions may apply. No endorsement of Anton by NASA or its partners is implied.

Image treatment

Daily imagery is resized using NASA’s image service, cropped to fill the content canvas, subdued with low opacity and a dark scrim, and overlaid with a static pixel mesh. These are aesthetic adaptations, not changes to the underlying science. The bundled fallback is additionally center-cropped to 16:9, reduced to 480 × 270 pixels, ordered-dithered and exported as a static 960 × 540 WebP. The interface does not animate either image.
